## judge reliability (8B SelfAskScaleScorer, clamped to [0,1])
# The LLM judge is noisy BOTH ways: TAP judged SUCCESS 5/10 with 0 real leaks (over-reports on

# real secret is the only trustworthy signal. Also had to CLAMP scale_value_float to [0,1] - the
# 8B judge sometimes rates below the rubric min, producing a negative score that crashes the node.
